A chess puzzle a day, in one bite.

Every day Chess Bites posts a short puzzle to solve, followed by a full video that walks through the solution and the tempting moves that fail.

Watch on YouTube

Solve it

A Short shows the position and gives you a few seconds to find the best move.

See why

The solution video explains the winning idea and why the obvious moves don't work.

Every day

A new puzzle each day, from beginner tactics to harder combinations.

Where the puzzles come from

Positions come from the public Lichess puzzle database (CC0). Every line is checked with the Stockfish chess engine. The videos are produced by a small program that the channel owner runs on their own computer, and no AI-generated images, voices or text are used.
